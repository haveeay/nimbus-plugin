NIMBUS - installing on macOS
============================

1. Double-click "Install Nimbus.pkg".

   macOS will say it can't verify the developer (this build isn't notarized
   by Apple). Click "Done", then open:
       System Settings > Privacy & Security
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to "Install Nimbus.pkg",
   then confirm with your password.

2. Follow the installer. It puts:
       Nimbus.vst3       in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
       Nimbus.component  in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
       Nimbus.app        in /Applications

3. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System
   Folders" and/or "Use Audio Units v2", then click Rescan.
   Nimbus is under Plug-Ins > Javi.

Your own presets live in ~/Library/Audio/Presets/Javi/Nimbus
(the FOLDER button in Nimbus opens it).

The full Owner's Manual is the PDF next to this file.
