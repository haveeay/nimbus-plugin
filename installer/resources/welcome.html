Nimbus __VERSION__

A vintage DCO pad synthesizer with tape echo, drive and the CLOUD granular section.

This installs:

  • VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins for Ableton Live and other DAWs
  • the Nimbus standalone app

Click Customize on the next step if you only want some of these.

Your saved presets are kept in ~/Library/Audio/Presets/Javi/Nimbus and are never touched by the installer.
